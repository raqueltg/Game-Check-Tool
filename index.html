How to complete the checklist

For each listed element, indicate whether it is present. The original Game-check asks researchers to check all applicable elements. In this digital version, each element has an individual response and optional notes.

How to interpret these results

The counts and charts below describe which checklist indicators were identified. They do not measure harm or establish that a game is safe or unsafe. Contextual evaluation and researcher judgement are needed before drawing conclusions. Wellbeing supportive measures are reported separately and do not cancel out pressure indicators.

Using the Game-check prototype

Complete the descriptive questions, then assess the listed elements under money, time and attention/engagement. Select one response per item: Yes, No or Unclear. Use the separate Further information button whenever you want to add an explanation, evidence or researcher notes.

Colours are used for navigation and visual distinction only; they do not indicate that an element is good or bad. For the maximum spending limit item (M03), use Further information to record the actual cap, where it was found and whether it is suitable for the player and their family. A cap being present does not automatically mean it supports wellbeing. The original tool notes that pressure strategies are not necessarily negative and that strategies may cover multiple domains. Some items are wellbeing supportive measures and are reported separately.

Response meanings

  • Yes: the element was identified.
  • No: the element was not identified.
  • Unclear: available information is insufficient to determine whether the element is present.

All data is stored in this browser on this computer. Screenshots and screen recordings are kept locally in this browser. Use Export complete package (ZIP) to download the assessment together with its evidence. This prototype has no server or user accounts.

Screen recordings: Upload an existing MP4 or WebM recording (maximum 50 MB and 60 seconds per file). The website does not record your screen.

Important: The results are descriptive and the contextual ratings are researcher judgements, not validated risk scores. Test and refine the evaluation guidance before using it for actual research.
